# BulkImg Studio v1.1.0-beta.5 — Beta

> Beta release: this contains newer changes that may disrupt your workflow. Stable is recommended for normal production use.

## Highlights

- Adds a persistent USD / PKR cost-display switch, with USD as the default.
- Refreshes the State Bank of Pakistan exchange-rate lookup every 15 minutes while the app is open.
- Makes laptop-height windows more compact while keeping the sidebar, output controls, and Generate rail in view.
- Restores clearer Usage tables and keeps pricing details open by default.

## Update test

From v1.1.0-beta.4, open **About**, enable **Receive beta updates**, then choose **Check for updates** and **Download and install** for v1.1.0-beta.5. The app should restart and show v1.1.0-beta.5 in About.
