# BulkImg Studio v1.1.0-beta.4 — Beta

> Beta release: this contains newer changes that may disrupt your workflow. Stable is recommended for normal production use.

## Highlights

- Carries the corrected automatic-update helper introduced in Beta 3.
- Published specifically to verify that a running Beta 3 app can download, replace itself, restart, and confirm healthy startup without manual installer steps.

## Update test

From v1.1.0-beta.3, open **About**, keep **Receive beta updates** enabled, then choose **Check for updates** and **Download and install** for v1.1.0-beta.4. The app should restart itself and show v1.1.0-beta.4 in About.
